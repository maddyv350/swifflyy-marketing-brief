# Swifflyy: Product & Brand Brief for Motion Graphics

> **Who this is for:** a Claude session that will design and produce marketing motion graphics
> (Reels/Shorts ads, launch films, app-store previews) for Swifflyy. It covers what the app
> is, who it's for, what you may and may not claim, the full visual identity, and an indexed
> set of real screenshots. The screenshots are attached separately as image files. Match them
> by the filenames in §9.
>
> Facts are current as of **3 Nov 2026**. Older docs that say "₹9/day", "Mapbox", or
> "smiling selfie" are out of date. Trust this one over them.

---

## 1. The one-liner

**Swifflyy is a dating app for right now.** You drop a pin where you actually are (a café, a
gig, a park), and **for the next hour** you're visible to people **within 1 km** of that
spot. Like each other, and a chat opens. You're already in the same place, so go say hi.

- **App Store name:** Swifflyy: Meet People Nearby
- **Subtitle / core tagline:** *Drop a pin. Meet who's near.*
- **Splash tagline (handwritten):** *drop a pin. meet nearby.*
- **Welcome-screen line:** *Someone nice is closer than you think.*
- **Website headline:** *Love, within walking distance.*
- **Short positioning:** *Meet people where you are.*

The name is spelled **Swifflyy**: two f's, two y's at the end. In the logo it's written in
lowercase: **swifflyy**.

## 2. The problem and the insight

**Problem.** Dating apps match you with people 40 km away. In Bangalore traffic that's a
different city. You chat for three weeks, nothing happens, and the app wants you to keep
swiping.

**Insight.** The one thing that actually predicts a first date is *being in the same place*.
So Swifflyy starts from place, not from an algorithm.

**Promise.** Fewer people, but real and close enough to meet in minutes. *Talk now, meet in
minutes.*

Lines already used in our marketing (reuse freely):

- "Your match is forty kilometres away. In Bangalore traffic."
- "Dating apps forgot the 'meet' part."
- "No endless swiping."
- "A deck, not a slot machine."
- "They're two tables away. Go say hi."
- "No creepy live tracking. One hour later? Poof."
- "chai date > cold DMs"
- "touch grass, meet people"
- "Love, locally."

## 3. How it works (the user journey, in order)

These are the beats a video should tell. Screenshot filenames are in brackets.

1. **Open the app.** The coral splash shows the pin logo bobbing over its rings and the
   wordmark. Then a full-bleed photo welcome: *"Someone nice is closer than you think."*
   [`app/splash_page.png`, `app/welcome_page.png`]
2. **Sign in with your phone.** Phone number, then a 6-digit SMS code. *"No passwords, ever."*
   [`app/login_page.png`, `app/otp_page.png`]
3. **Make a quick profile in 8 steps.** Name, email, birthday (napkin scroll wheels), gender,
   who you'd like to date, dating intention, up to 6 photos, and up to 3 written **prompts**
   (Hinge-style: *"The way to win me over is…"*). *"Prompts, not essays."*
   [`app/onboarding_birthday.png`, `app/onboarding_who_to_date.png`,
   `app/prompts_step_filled.png`, `app/profile_finishing_done.png`]
4. **Get verified with one selfie.** It's matched against your profile photos and earns you a
   ✓ verified badge. The selfie stays private and is never shown to anyone.
   [`store/06-verified-by-selfie.png`]
5. **DROP: drop a pin.** On the map (DROP tab), the pin sits on where you physically are.
   Pick what you're there for (coffee, drinks, wandering, working, live music, spontaneous,
   celebrating, new in town) and tap **"Drop it here."** The pin is live for **1 hour**, then
   expires on its own. [`store/01-drop-a-pin.png`]
6. **DISCOVER: see who's around.** A deck of people within **1 km** of your pin, each card
   showing their distance (e.g. "0.4 km"). Swipe right to like, left to pass. A sketched heart
   pops or an ink cross draws itself as each card leaves. [`store/02-see-whos-around.png`]
7. **It's a match.** When you both like each other, two polaroid-style photos tilt toward
   each other with a coral heart, and *"it's a match!"* appears in handwriting. **Say hello**
   or **keep swiping**. [`store/03-its-a-match.png`, `app/match_screen.png`]
8. **CHAT: talk now, meet in minutes.** Real-time chat with typing dots and push
   notifications. The chat in our screenshots: *"wait, you dropped at Church Street too?" ·
   "no way, I'm two tables away" · "come say hi already"* [`store/04-talk-now.png`]
9. **LIKES: see who already liked you.** A separate tab.
10. **After you meet.** In a chat you can tap **We met** (a little positive signal), or
    **Unmatch** / **Report & unmatch**. Both are one tap.

Bottom navigation, five tabs: **DROP · DISCOVER · LIKES · CHAT · PROFILE**.

### Swifflyy Plus (the paid tier)

The core app is free: dropping, discovering, matching and chatting all cost nothing.
**Plus** adds three perks:

1. **Unlimited drops**, instead of 3 a day
2. **2 km reach**, instead of 1 km
3. **Top of the deck**: people at your spot see your card first

| Plan | Price | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Pass | **₹29** | 24 hours |
| Weekly Plan | **₹49** | 7 days ("best value") |

Both are **one-time purchases, never auto-renewing**. Buy again and the time **stacks**.
Positioning: *"Priced for India: no bloated monthly subscription doing the guilt-tripping."*
A good joke: ₹29 is about the price of a cutting chai. [`store/08-swifflyy-plus.png`]

## 5. Safety & trust (a real selling point; don't overstate it)

- **Place-based, not GPS-tracked.** Your location is shared only while a pin *you* dropped is
  live. No background tracking. If you leave, the pin stays where you dropped it until it
  expires.
- **It expires.** Every pin vanishes after an hour. Nothing stays on a map.
- **Fake GPS gets bounced.** Mock locations and impossible travel are detected and rejected,
  so "400 m away" really is 400 m away.
- **Selfie verification.** One selfie is matched to your photos and earns a ✓ badge. You can
  filter your deck to **verified people only**.
- **One tap to end it.** Unmatch or Report & unmatch, right from the chat.

## 6. Do NOT claim (these are untrue or out of date)

- ❌ "₹9 a day". The price is **₹29/day** or **₹49/week**.
- ❌ "Live" or "smiling" selfie, or liveness detection. It's one plain selfie matched to photos.
- ❌ "Every profile is verified." Verification is **optional** (the badge is a filter, not a gate).
- ❌ "Chat shows where you matched / remembers the spot." Not built.
- ❌ "Friends who join with your link jump the line." No referral mechanism exists.
- ❌ "Live in six cities." It's launching first on Church Street, Bengaluru (§11).
- ❌ Any feature-level AI or "smart matching algorithm." The deck is sorted by proximity,
  with Plus users first.
- ❌ Comparisons that name competitors on screen (Tinder/Bumble/Hinge) in paid ads. Keep it
  to "other dating apps" unless told otherwise.

## 7. Audience & tone

**Who:** urban Indians aged roughly **20–32**, starting with Bengaluru (Church Street, MG Road,
Indiranagar and Koramangala crowds). They already use Hinge, Tinder or Bumble and are tired of
it. They go to cafés, gigs and breweries and are on Instagram Reels. Gen-Z and young
millennials.

**Tone:** warm, playful, a little cheeky, never cringe. It should sound like a friend telling
you about an app, not an announcer. Lowercase handwritten asides ("really this close ✦",
"two tables away, really"). Indian English is natural, and light Hinglish works for ads
("chai se bhi sasta").

**Feel:** *spontaneous, local, real-life, low-pressure.* It's dating that ends in an actual
hello, not a pen-pal.

## 8. Visual identity: the "napkin sketch" (non-negotiable)

Everything should look like **it was doodled on a café napkin**: cream paper, ink-pen lines,
and **one** coral accent. This is the shipped app's real look, not a style suggestion. A
previous dark, 3D, gradient version of our website was rejected outright.

### Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#FBF8F1` | Main background (cream) |
| Paper 2 | `#F3EEE2` | Cards, fills |
| Ink | `#1F1D1B` | Text, line work. Also used as a full "ink" background for contrast slides |
| Ink 2 | `#3A3733` | Secondary text |
| Muted | `#7A766F` | Hints, captions |
| Faint | `#BBB6AC` | Dividers |
| Sketch line | `#2A2724` | ~1.5px hand-drawn borders |
| **Coral (accent)** | **`#D94F3A`** | The brand color: CTAs, pin, hearts, underlines, splash bg, app icon |
| Coral soft | `#F2C9C0` | Tints, chips, soft backgrounds |

### Type (font files are in `brand/fonts/`)

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines / display | **Bricolage Grotesque** (Bold/ExtraBold) | Chunky and tight, e.g. "Drop a **pin** where you are" |
| Body / UI | **Hanken Grotesk** | Clean, neutral |
| Handwritten | **Caveat** (Regular/Bold only) | Wordmark-style moments, annotations, asides, button labels in caps with wide tracking ("SAY HELLO", "DROP IT HERE") |

Headline pattern from the store screenshots: an ink Bricolage headline where **one key word is
coral** and has a **hand-drawn coral squiggle underline** beneath it. A small Caveat "kicker"
line sits above (e.g. *"no endless swiping —"*).

### Logo & icon

- **Mark:** a cream **map pin with a round hole**, sitting on **two concentric elliptical
  rings** (a "ripple" where it lands), on a coral rounded square. [`brand/app-icon-1024.png`]
- **Wordmark:** lowercase **swifflyy**, either chunky (Bricolage, on the splash) or
  handwritten (Caveat, on the welcome screen, with a coral underline stroke).
- **Splash:** coral background, cream pin and rings, cream wordmark, Caveat tagline *"drop a
  pin. meet nearby."*, and **"MADE IN BENGALURU"** at the foot. [`app/splash_page.png`]

### Illustration & doodles

- Hand-drawn **ink doodles** only: hearts, sparkles ✦, crosses ✕, smileys, chat bubbles,
  arrows, dotted lines, tape strips, squiggles. Lines are thin and slightly wobbly.
- The app has its own icon set of **two-tone ink doodles** with coral accents (wine, coffee,
  pin, heart, confetti, music, bolt, tree…). [`brand/sketch-icons.png`]
- Marketing uses an **illustrated cast** of fictional people (flat, friendly portraits:
  Aanya, Dev, Meher, Arjun, Tara, Zoya, Kabir, Harman). You can see them in the store
  screenshots. Use illustrated people or clearly-licensed stock, **never real users**.
- Phones are shown as **hand-drawn phone outlines**, slightly tilted (±1.3°), with real UI
  inside. Never a photoreal 3D device render.
- Cards and buttons have a **hard offset "sketch" shadow** (a solid ink shadow a few px
  down-right, no blur).

### Hard rules

- **No OS emoji, ever.** Hearts, pins and sparkles are drawn doodles. (✦ ✓ as text glyphs are
  fine.)
- **Light, paper-first.** No dark-mode UI, no glassmorphism, no neon, no 3D, no glossy
  gradients. A solid ink-black slide or a soft coral wash as a background is fine, because the
  store screenshots alternate cream / ink / coral-soft.
- **One accent color.** Coral is the only saturated color, apart from the people's skin, hair
  and clothes.

## 10. Motion language (how the app itself moves; mirror it)

- **Things get drawn into existence.** Pencil and ink strokes write themselves, and underlines
  self-draw beneath the key word. Transitions are scribble-outs, paper flips, page tears and
  ink bleeds. Avoid generic slides, fades or zoom punches.
- **Easing:** nothing linear. Entrances ease-out-cubic, exits ease-in-cubic, playful pops
  ease-out-back (slight overshoot). Sibling elements stagger by about **50 ms** and rise in
  about **10 px** as they fade in.
- **Timing tokens:** micro 240 ms · standard 320 ms · page 380 ms.
- **The swipe (signature beat, 960 ms):** the card fades out while drifting the way it was
  flung. An **ink ✕ draws itself in two strokes** for a pass, or a **sketched heart pops in**
  with overshoot for a like. It floats up, holds, shrinks away, and the next card slides up
  96 px.
- **The match:** two polaroids tilt in and **pop** (springy), a coral heart lands between
  them, *"it's a match!"* is handwritten, and soft coral hearts float up.
- **The pin drop:** the pin falls, lands with a **coral ripple** (the two rings from the
  logo), and the "1 hr" timer appears. The logo's rings are literally this ripple, so it's
  the strongest brand motif. Use it for logo reveals.
- **Splash:** the pin **bobs** gently over its pulsing rings.
- **Sound (optional):** pencil scratches, paper flips, a soft pop on the match, a warm lo-fi
  or indie-acoustic bed (~90–112 BPM). For VO, a warm, young Indian-English voice.

## 11. Launch context

- **Status:** pre-launch. swifflyy.com shows a "dropping soon" teaser with a waitlist, and
  **swifflyy.com/waitlist** lets people save a spot or set up their profile in the browser
  ahead of launch.
- **First launch:** **Church Street, Bengaluru, in December 2026.** This is a
  neighbourhood-first launch, so the map is never empty.
- **Later:** city by city. The website mentions Chandigarh, Delhi NCR, Lucknow, Mumbai and
  Pune as next.
- **CTA options:** "Join the waitlist at swifflyy.com" (pre-launch) or "Download Swifflyy"
  (post-launch). Store: App Store + Google Play, **India only**.
- **Contact / handle:** hello@swifflyy.com · support@swifflyy.com · swifflyy.com
